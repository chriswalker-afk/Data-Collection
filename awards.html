How these awards work

Checked awards use first-attempt or unassisted answers from Solo games. Hints, revealed models, corrections and ungraded written work never count as verified accuracy. Confidence ratings and substantial independent writing have their own clearly labelled practice awards. Solo progress and trophies stay on this device unless you export them. A restored Solo backup is not independently authenticated, and there is no cross-device account. Multiplayer keepsakes are recorded separately from live, server-graded results when the ceremony reaches its finale. Resetting or restoring your Solo cabinet does not change Multiplayer keepsakes, saved Solo progress or exam writing.
